OwnerMaclean Majeski

Maclean Majeski (Owner)

In 2015, my brother and I began mowing lawns for a few of our neighbors. Since then, our work has extended to dozens of customers across Northern Colorado (and funded a year of college along the way). My goal is to alleviate the burden of yard maintenance and give my customers a chance to simply enjoy their lawn. In everything from providing quotes, to billing, to lawn care and snow removal, I’ll make it easy for you.
